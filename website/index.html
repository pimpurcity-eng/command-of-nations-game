Keep defending.

Set Guard mode to automate defence while you're away. In online matches, an AI caretaker can also take over an absent nation and hand command back when you return.

EXPLORE THE GAME

Every part of your
nation matters.

A connected campaign of warfare, economics, trade and diplomacy. Explore the systems that shape your decisions.

Land, air & sea

Command infantry, armour, artillery, helicopters, aircraft, drones, warships and submarines on a 3D campaign map.

An arsenal that evolves

Research five equipment tiers, unlock new weapons as the campaign advances and develop your nation's doctrine.

Win through your economy

Build industry, manage food, fuel, materials, electronics and manpower, and pursue a dedicated economic victory.

Real trade routes

Connect trading partners with trains, container ships and oil tankers. Cargo deliveries grow your national income.

Diplomacy has consequences

Negotiate peace, trade agreements, right of way and shared vision. Form coalitions, declare war or impose an embargo.

Guard your nation

Use Guard mode for automated defence while you are away. Online matches also give absent nations an AI caretaker.

Build your nation's future

Develop cities and provinces with factories, airbases, naval bases, hospitals, support depots, bunkers and railway stations.

Persistent online play

Join 2-player Russia vs Ukraine with your commander account. The server runs the match, remembers your nation and enforces fog of war.

A deeper battlefield

Use ranged artillery, air patrols, layered air defence, radar, stealth, entrenchment, retreat and strategic missile weapons.

Choose your route to victory

Compete through domination, economic strength, elimination or victory points. Your strategy can reach beyond conquest.

Know what happens

Read the war newspaper, battle and Guard reports, track army details and respond to attack alerts.

Grow as a commander

Follow achievements, medals, ranks and account progression. Six interface languages and a first-play tutorial help you get started.
