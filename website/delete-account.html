Warfront Mandate: WW3

Delete your account

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Warfront Mandate and tap More → Player account.
  2. Sign in if needed, then tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account forever.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the game

Email support@warfrontmandate.com from the email address of your account with the subject Delete my account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your email address, password, commander name, level, XP, games played, wins, losses and the record of your finished games. Nothing is kept, except where the law requires it.

What is not affected

Campaign progress saved on your phone stays on your phone. Uninstalling the game removes it.
