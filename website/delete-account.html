Delete your Warfront Mandate account

You can request the deletion of your Warfront Mandate game account and associated data without needing to install or open the game.

How to request deletion

  1. Use the button below to email our support team.
  2. State that you want to delete your Warfront Mandate account and include the email address associated with your game account (or your player username, if applicable).
  3. We may ask you to verify that the account belongs to you before processing the request.

Request account deletion by email

Or email support@warfrontmandate.com directly. Never send your password.

What will be deleted?

Once the request is verified and processed, the game account and personal data associated with it, such as profile information and saved progress, will be deleted. Some limited information may need to be kept where required for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons. If any information must be retained, we will explain what and why.

Questions about a deletion request?

Contact support@warfrontmandate.com.
