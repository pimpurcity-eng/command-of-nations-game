# In-match music credits (battle_theme_2 … battle_theme_5)

All four tracks are instrumental (no vocals). Common processing for every file
(ffmpeg): trimmed to the musical start and end, two-pass EBU R128 loudness
normalisation to -18 LUFS integrated (true peak at or below -1.5 dBTP before
encoding), short fade-in and fade-out for clean looping, resampled to 44.1 kHz
stereo, encoded as Ogg Vorbis q5, source metadata stripped.

## battle_theme_2.ogg — "Drum Cadence"

- Composer: military drum cadence written by U.S. Army band musicians (Commons
  credits Ed Teleky, Mark Carson and Sheila Klotz). As a work of U.S. federal
  government employees it is public domain.
- Performer: U.S. military band percussion section. The archive.org copy credits
  the 82nd Airborne Division Band (U.S. Army). An identical-length recording on
  Wikimedia Commons is credited to the U.S. Air Force Band Concert Band (*Esprit
  de Corps*, 1997). Either way it is a U.S. military band recording.
- Source: https://archive.org/details/DrumCadence (file `drum_cadence.ogg`)
  - Commons copy, same recording: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drum_Cadence_-_Concert_Band_-_United_States_Air_Force_Band.mp3
- Licence: Public domain (work of the U.S. federal government / U.S. military;
  Commons tags `PD-USGov-Military-Army` / `PD-USGov-Military-Air Force` for the
  composition, performance and recording). No attribution required. Credit
  given as a courtesy.
- Edits: trimmed 1.55 s to 182.30 s (removed leading and trailing silence);
  normalised to -18 LUFS; 0.4 s fade-in and 2.5 s fade-out; 44.1 kHz stereo
  Vorbis q5. Length 3:01.
