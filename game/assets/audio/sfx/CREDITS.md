# Sound effect credits
All files are real recordings (or mixes of real recordings), trimmed, faded, resampled to 44.1 kHz, loudness-normalised and encoded as Ogg Vorbis q5.
US-government works (DVIDS / US military) are public domain in the United States (17 U.S.C. 105). DVIDS pages state they are free of known copyright restrictions; please do not imply endorsement by the U.S. Department of Defense.
