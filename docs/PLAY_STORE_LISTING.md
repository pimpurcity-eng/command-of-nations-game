## Content rating (IARC questionnaire)

Answer truthfully in Play Console. For the current game:
- Violence: **yes**, realistic military vehicles and explosions; no people shown, no blood or gore.
- Fear/horror, sexuality, gambling, crude humour, drugs: **no**.
- Users interact: **yes once online play opens** (chat, leaderboard with commander names). Single-player chat is with the AI only.
- Shares location: **no**. Digital purchases: **no**.

Expected result: about **Teen / PEGI 12** (decided by the questionnaire, not by us).

## App access (for Google's reviewers)

The game is fully playable without an account. To review the account screens, give a test
account (email + password) in **App content → App access**.

## Account deletion

**App content → Data deletion:** in-app path *More → Player account → Delete account*; web URL
`https://warfrontmandate.com/delete-account.html`.

## Testing track

New personal developer accounts must run a **closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days in
a row** before applying for production. Add testers by email list or Google Group, keep them opted
in for the full 14 days.
