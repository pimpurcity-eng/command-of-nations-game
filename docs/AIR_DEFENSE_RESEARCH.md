# Air defense research

Two independent branches, five levels each. Level 1 starts available; later levels require the previous level, resources, a free research slot and the day shown.

| Level | Day | Short range | Range / AA damage | Long range | Range / AA damage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Strela-10 | 2.4 / 7 | Buk-M3 | 3.5 / 10 |
| 2 | 1 | Strela-10 guidance | 2.5 / 8 | Buk-M3 radar | 3.8 / 11.5 |
| 3 | 2 | Tor-M2 | 2.7 / 9 | S-400 Triumf | 4.2 / 13 |
| 4 | 4 | Tor-M2 radar | 2.8 / 10 | S-400 guidance | 4.5 / 14.5 |
| 5 | 6 | Tor-M2 interception | 3.0 / 11 | S-500 Prometey | 5.0 / 16 |

Ranges are game units. Both branches currently engage aircraft and drones. Missile and satellite defense is outside this implementation. Recovered Skyguard and Patriot meshes remain labelled stand-ins. Existing armies need manual modernization; new production uses the researched level.
